# Privacy Policy for TripSphere

**Last Updated: August 03, 2026**

TripSphere ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

## 1. Information Collection
We do not require users to create an account or provide personal identification information (such as name, email address, or phone number) to use the core features of the App.

### Information Collected Automatically
When you use the App, certain information may be collected automatically, including:
- **Device Information:** We may collect information about your mobile device, such as device model, operating system version, and unique device identifiers.
- **Usage Data:** We may collect information about how you interact with the App, including the features you use and the time spent on the App.

## 2. Third-Party Services
The App integrates with several third-party services to provide travel information, booking capabilities, and advertisements. These third parties have their own privacy policies:

- **Google AdMob:** We use AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and use data (such as your advertising ID) to personalize ads and analyze performance. 
    - [Google's Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- **Klook / Skyscanner / Agoda / Trip.com:** The App includes widgets and links to these affiliated travel services. When you interact with these widgets or click on booking links, these third parties may collect information according to their own privacy policies.
- **WebView Content:** Some parts of the App use WebViews to display external travel content. These WebViews may store cookies on your device to enhance your browsing experience.

## 3. Use of Information
The information we collect is used to:
- Provide and maintain the App's functionality.
- Improve and personalize your user experience.
- Display relevant advertisements.
- Analyze usage patterns to improve App performance.

## 4. Data Security
We take reasonable measures to protect any information collected automatically from unauthorized access or disclosure. However, please be aware that no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

## 5. Children's Privacy
The App is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.

## 7. Contact Us
If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at support@fintratech.com.
