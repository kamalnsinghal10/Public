# Privacy Policy for Wizard Chess 3D

**Effective Date:** November 24, 2026  
**Application:** Wizard Chess 3D (`com.wizardchess.sanctum`)  
**Developer Contact:** [KAMALSINGHAL10@gmail.com](mailto:KAMALNSINGHAL10@gmail.com)

---

## 1. Overview
Wizard Chess 3D is an offline-capable single-player 3D chess game with local Stockfish move analysis. Your privacy is respected by design: the app does not require user accounts, does not display advertisements, and does not collect, sell, or share personal information.

---

## 2. Information We Do Not Collect
Wizard Chess 3D does **not** collect, store on external servers, or transmit:
* Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or user account credentials
* Location data (GPS, Wi-Fi, or cell-tower location)
* Photos, camera, microphone, contacts, files, or device identifiers
* Advertising IDs, behavioral analytics, or crash-tracking telemetry

---

## 3. On-Device Processing & Local Storage
All gameplay logic, 3D rendering, AI opponent calculations, PGN move imports, and Stockfish chess analysis run **entirely locally on your device**.
* Any chess moves or PGN notation you enter remain on your device and are never uploaded to any server.
* When used in a web browser or installed as a Progressive Web App (PWA), standard browser storage/cache may keep static 3D assets and game engine files locally on your device so the app works offline. No tracking cookies are used.

---

## 4. Network Access & Third-Party Services
* **Offline Functionality:** Wizard Chess 3D does not rely on any backend server to play chess or analyze games.
* **Google Fonts (Optional Online Typography):** When your device is connected to the internet, the app may request web font files from Google Fonts (`fonts.googleapis.com` and `fonts.gstatic.com`). Like any standard web request, Google may process basic connection metadata (such as your IP address and user-agent) in accordance with [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). The app includes no third-party ad networks, social SDKs, or analytics trackers.

---

## 5. Children's Privacy
Because Wizard Chess 3D does not collect personal data from any user, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction).

---

## 6. Security
The Android application blocks unencrypted cleartext HTTP network traffic (`usesCleartextTraffic="false"`) and disables automatic Android cloud backups (`allowBackup="false"`).
