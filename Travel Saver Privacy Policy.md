# Privacy Policy for TravelSaver: Anti-Price Hike Shredder

**Last Updated:** November 2026

TravelSaver: Anti-Price Hike Shredder ("TravelSaver", "we", "us", or "our") is 100% free and dedicated to protecting user privacy.

## 1. Single Purpose & Core Functionality
TravelSaver has one purpose: to protect consumers from dynamic price hikes on airline, hotel, and travel booking platforms by deleting tracking cookies, local storage, session storage, IndexedDB, and CacheStorage strictly associated with the active booking website when requested by the user.

## 2. Data We Access & Why
- **Active Tab Domain & URL (`activeTab`):** Accessed ephemerally solely when you open the extension popup and click to clean trackers.
- **Cookies (`cookies` & `host_permissions`):** Required by Chrome to inspect and remove cookies strictly matching the active website's domain and parent domain (e.g., `.united.com`). Cookies belonging to unrelated domains are filtered out and never touched. TravelSaver never reads or monitors sites in the background.
- **On-Page Storage (`scripting`):** Clears `localStorage`, `sessionStorage`, `indexedDB`, and `CacheStorage` on the active tab frame only when you trigger a clean.
- **Local Preferences (`storage`):** Minimal local shred counts stored strictly in `chrome.storage.local` on your device.

## 3. Data We DO NOT Collect
- Zero browsing history, zero personally identifiable information, zero telemetry, zero remote analytics, and zero account/payment profiles.

## 4. Optional Support ("Buy Me a Coffee")
TravelSaver is completely free with unlimited use. If you optionally choose to send a voluntary coffee contribution via PayPal, PayPal handles the transaction directly on paypal.com. TravelSaver never collects or stores payment information.
