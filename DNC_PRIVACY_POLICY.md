# Privacy Policy for Do Nothing Club

**Last Updated: July 30, 2026**

This privacy policy explains how **Do Nothing Club** ("we," "our," or "the App") handles your information. By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Personal Information
- **Username:** When you complete a "Do Nothing" session, you may choose to provide a username to be displayed on the global leaderboard.
- **Unique Identifier:** We generate and store a persistent, random Unique ID (UUID) on your device. This ID is used solely to associate your scores with your account on our global leaderboard and does not contain any personally identifiable information from your device (such as your name, email, or phone number).

### B. Usage Data
- We collect data regarding the duration of your "Do Nothing" sessions to provide the leaderboard functionality.

## 2. Third-Party Services
The App uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you:

- **Google AdMob:** We use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and use data such as your device's advertising ID for personalized advertising. You can learn more about how Google uses data [here](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

## 3. Data Retention and Security
- We store usernames and session durations on our backend (Google Apps Script) to provide the leaderboard.
- We take reasonable measures to protect your information, but please be aware that no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

## 4. Children's Privacy
Our App does not specifically target children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children.

## 5. Your Rights
You can choose not to provide a username. In this case, your scores will be associated with your anonymous Unique ID.

## 6. Contact Us
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact the developer via the GitHub repository or the contact information provided in the Play Store listing.
