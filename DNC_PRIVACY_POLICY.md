# Privacy Policy for Do Nothing Club

**Last Updated: November 25, 2026**

This privacy policy explains how **Do Nothing Club** ("we," "our," or "the App") handles your information. By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Leaderboard Identity
- **Display Name:** When you complete a stillness session, you may optionally enter a display name to appear on the Global Club leaderboard.
- **Anonymous Identifier:** We generate and store a random Unique ID (UUID) locally on your device. This ID is used solely to keep track of your personal best score on the leaderboard and does not contain any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email, or phone number).

### B. Session Data
- **On-Device History:** Your session logs, streaks, and Zen level progression stay stored locally on your device.
- **Global Best Duration:** Only your chosen display name, anonymous UUID, and best session duration (in seconds) are transmitted when submitting to the Global Club leaderboard.

## 2. Third-Party Services
The App uses the following third-party services:

- **Cloudflare Workers & KV:** Hosts our global leaderboard API and stores leaderboard rankings (display name, anonymous UUID, and best session duration).
- **Google AdMob:** Displays interstitial and optional rewarded ads. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID) for ad delivery and measurement. Learn more in [Google's Advertising Policy](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).
- **Google Play Billing:** Processes optional one-time in-app purchases (such as the Cosmos Meteor sanctuary theme). Payment details are handled exclusively by Google Play; we never receive or store your financial information.

## 3. Data Retention & Security
- Leaderboard data is transmitted over HTTPS and retains only your best score and chosen display name.
- You may participate without setting a display name (in which case your anonymous UUID or "Anonymous" is used).

## 4. Children's Privacy
Our App is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 5. Contact Us
If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us via the developer support email listed on the Google Play Store page.
